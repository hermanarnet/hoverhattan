Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026

The short version: Hoverhattan does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress on your iPhone: coins, garage items, missions, your best score and your sound setting. This data stays on your device and is never sent to me or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

Game Center

If you're signed in to Apple's Game Center, your best score is submitted to the Hoverhattan leaderboard so you can compare it with other players. Game Center is run by Apple and covered by Apple's Privacy Policy. I don't receive or store your Game Center details. You can turn off Game Center in the iPhone's Settings app at any time.

Network

The game is bundled in the app and runs offline. It doesn't load content from the internet and doesn't contact any server run by me or by third parties.

Children

Hoverhattan is suitable for all ages and doesn't collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
