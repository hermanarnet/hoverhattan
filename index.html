Support

Hoverhattan is an arcade game for iPhone: fly a hovercar up the avenues of a neon Manhattan, dodge towers and sky-cabs, chain near misses and collect coins to upgrade your ride.

Need help or found a bug?

Email herman.arnet@gmail.com. Tell me your iPhone model and iOS version, and what happened. I usually reply within a few days.

How do I play?

Drag anywhere to steer. Fly close past obstacles to score near misses: they charge your boost. When the boost meter is full, double-tap to fire it. Use the Roll buttons for a barrel roll left or right.

There's no sound

Hoverhattan follows your iPhone's silent switch, so it can play alongside your own music. Turn the silent switch off and check the speaker button on the title screen.

Where is my progress saved?

Coins, garage items, missions and your best score are saved on your iPhone. They are not stored online, so deleting the app also deletes your progress.

The leaderboard doesn't show up

The leaderboard uses Apple's Game Center. Sign in to Game Center in the iPhone's Settings app, then open Hoverhattan again.
